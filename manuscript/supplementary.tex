\section*{Extended Methods}
\subsection*{Bilinear Interpolation of Magnetic Field Data}
The crustal magnetic field data sourced from Hood et al. (2020) and Hood et al. (2022) were provided on a \SI{0.5}{\degree} resolution grid. To estimate the magnetic field magnitude at specific landing site coordinates, a bilinear interpolation algorithm was implemented. This approach utilizes the four nearest grid points to the target coordinate $(\lambda, \phi)$, weighting the contribution of each point inversely by its geographic distance to the target. The magnetic field components $(B_r, B_\theta, B_\phi)$ were interpolated independently before calculating the total magnitude $|\mathbf{B}|$.

\subsection*{Coordinate Database Compilation}
Landing site coordinates were compiled from peer-reviewed mission reports and NASA planetary data archives. For the Artemis III candidate sites, coordinates were sourced from the NASA Artemis III Candidate Landing Regions report (2022). Historical Apollo site coordinates were verified against the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC) derived coordinates.
